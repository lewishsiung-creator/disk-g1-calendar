# Grade 1 at DISK · 2026–2027

A bilingual (English + 繁體中文) month-grid version of the Dominican
International School Kaohsiung calendar for School Year 2026–2027, for Grade 1
families.

**Live:** https://disk-g1-calendar.pages.dev

It answers the three questions a calendar on the wall makes you hunt for:

- **Formal Uniform** — the 12 days read off the Mass, Assembly and Graduation
  entries. The school calendar prints no uniform rules anywhere, so this is a
  reading of the schedule, not a quotation from it. **School notice always wins.**
- **No school** — 32 days, including the four "No Students" staff days
  (Oct 12, Dec 18, Jan 5, Mar 19) that are easy to miss because they are not
  public holidays.
- **Exams and key dates** — examinations, assessments, report cards, and the
  days a parent attends or plans around.

There is also an `.ics` you can add to a phone calendar. Every event carries an
alarm at **20:00 the evening before**, so a Formal Uniform day surfaces the
night before rather than at breakfast.

## Accuracy

Transcribed from the school's PDF, revision "as of July 29, 2026", and checked
against the school's own printed school-day totals: counting weekdays minus
holidays reproduces Quarter 1 (42), Quarter 2 (48) and Quarter 3 (42) exactly.
Quarter 4 comes out one day short of the printed 48; the difference is Mar 19,
which the school appears to count as a school day. The printed figures are what
the page displays.

Two readings of the source worth knowing:

- **AP Examinations run May 3–14**, not May 14 alone.
- **There are two Parent-Teacher Conferences**: Nov 16–20 and Apr 19–23. The
  November one is written on an unnumbered continuation line under Career
  Awareness Week, which makes it easy to miss.
- **Dec 5** is shaded as a SAT Weekend on the printed grid but is not written in
  December's list. It is a genuine College Board test date, so it is shown here,
  marked with a †.

The school notes that it revises the calendar from time to time to match
government-mandated holidays. Always check against the latest version the school
sends home.

## Files

`index.html` is the page, `data.js` is every event. Both are generated — edit
them in the source project, not here.
